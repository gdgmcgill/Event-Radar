<objective>
Synthesise every register, capture, and report produced by this phase into the three deliverables a reviewer actually reads — three one-page threat models, the finding register in both representations, and the artifact index — then close the phase with the two gates that prove the audit did not contaminate what it audited.

Purpose: This is the phase's exit gate. A finding with no evidence gets contested and deprioritised; a finding with no validation criterion cannot be closed, so Stage 3 and Stage 4 would have nothing to prove against. And an audit that quietly modified the codebase or committed a secret has failed regardless of what it found.
Output: three threat models, `findings.json`, `FOUNDATION_AUDIT.md`, the consolidated redaction ledger, and the finalised artifact index.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

> The true current state of the codebase is known with captured evidence — every endpoint, page, policy, dependency, and risk inventoried, and every finding recorded with a reproduction and a validation criterion — without a single source, config, dependency, or database change.

## Trust Boundaries

| audit artifacts → git history | Committing is permanent; a leaked credential cannot be undone by deleting the file |

| audit process → audited codebase | The audit must leave the thing it measured unchanged |

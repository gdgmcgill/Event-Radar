DECISION
================================================================

Decision rule: the target is the newest PUBLISHED STABLE release on the 16.x line
that is at or above the highest first_patched version across every advisory
affecting a 16.x range. If a newer patched 16.3.x had been published since the
research document was written, THAT would be the target, not the number the plan
names. The plan can be stale; the registry cannot.

Applying the rule to the captures above:
  highest_first_patched_across_16x_advisories=16.3.3   (GHSA-2xp9-vwfh-vxw4, GHSA-p293-qw3h-jr36)
  newest_published_stable_16x=16.3.5                   (published 2026-09-11T17:26:46.049Z)
  registry_latest_dist_tag=16.3.5                      (not a 17.x — major-line hard stop not triggered)
  eslint_config_next_latest=16.3.5                     (lockstep: same version as next)
  16.3.5 >= 16.3.3                                     -> the target closes both criticals

target_next_version=16.3.5

Hard stop 1 — major line. NOT TRIGGERED. The registry 'latest' dist-tag is 16.3.5,
a 16.x. Had it been 17.x, the target would have stayed on the newest 16.x line: a
framework major is out of this phase's scope and would need its own change with a
migration note (STAB-10).

Hard stop 2 — unclosable advisory. NOT TRIGGERED. Every advisory with a 16.x
vulnerable range reports a first_patched at or below 16.3.3, and 16.3.5 is published.
No advisory requires a version that does not exist on the 16.x line, so there is no
blocking finding and no upgrade into a version that fails to close its own advisory.

Corroboration from this tree's own audit: npm reports the vulnerable range as
'9.3.4-canary.0 - 16.3.2', which is consistent with 16.3.3 being the first patched
release and therefore with 16.3.5 clearing it.

Requirement-text note: STAB-05 names 'the patched release closing the July-2026 CVE
batch' = 16.2.11. The captures above show eleven July-2026 advisories patched at
16.2.11 AND two 2026-09-08 criticals patched only at 16.3.3. 16.2.11 would leave both
criticals open. The requirement text is stale; the correction is recorded in
evidence/next-upgrade-note.md rather than applied silently.

Supabase client cast census — REFAC-04's cast clause, before removal
Plan: 03-06 · Phase: 03-refactor-foundations-schema-truth-and-the-seam-kit · Recorded: 2026-09-15

REFAC-04's clause is "(supabase as any) = 0". It is NOT "as any = 0". Two numbers
are recorded here so a later reader cannot mistake one for the other: the 47
client casts that are in scope, and the 61 casts of any kind that exist under
src/. The other 14 are `any` in mock factories and local casts, and 03-RESEARCH.md
§ Code Examples 7 puts them explicitly out of scope.

The distinction matters because a client cast is categorically worse than a local
one: `(supabase as any)` erases the WHOLE client type, including the ARGUMENT
types of every chained method. That is why removing them surfaces two real bugs
rather than none — see evidence/type-fixes-note.md.

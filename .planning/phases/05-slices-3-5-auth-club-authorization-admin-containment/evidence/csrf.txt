## 1. What changed

- src/server/csrf.ts (new): `isCrossSiteMutation(req)` and `crossSiteBlocked()`, with a docblock
  citing DEC-52, F-090 and Next's Server Action origin rule. The request's host is
  `x-forwarded-host` (first entry), else `host`, else `nextUrl.host`, lower-cased. The origin is
  parsed with `new URL(origin).host` inside try/catch; there is no regex.
- src/proxy.ts: immediately after the rate-limit statements, before the env reads and client
  creation:
      if (isCrossSiteMutation(request)) return crossSiteBlocked();
  The docblock's first line now lists the check. PROTECTED_ROUTES stays a one-line literal and the
  matcher is unchanged. Nothing was added between createServerClient and getUser.
- src/server/__tests__/csrf.test.ts (new, 41 cases).
- e2e/specs/csrf-origin.spec.ts: the two `DEFECT F-090` tests are now `FIXED F-090` and assert
  403 `{"error":"Cross-site request blocked"}` with no saved row (a new `postAndExpectBlocked`
  helper, with the same unsave-before and unsave-in-finally discipline). The docblock says what is
  fixed. The two PRESERVE tests and their helper are unchanged.
- evidence/csrf-assessment.md (new): REFAC-17's written assessment.
- evidence/defect-ledger.md: two F-090 rows.

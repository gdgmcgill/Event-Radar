## 6. Deviations recorded for Task 2

1. events/[id]/appeal keeps its pre-read on the door, with its own REGISTRY row. The plan names
   the events update and the review for the cookie client and does not place the pre-read. On the
   cookie client a non-creator cannot see a rejected or suspended event, so the route's
   403 {"error":"Forbidden"} would become 404 {"error":"Event not found"}: a wire change with no
   finding behind it. The pre-read therefore stays privileged; the routing suite pins the 403 and
   that no cookie-client call happens on the non-creator path.
2. The suggestions row does not say "follows". The caller's own user_follows and the club
   follower lists are world-readable ("Anyone can view follows", "Anyone can view follower data"),
   so those reads moved to the cookie client and the row names only what still needs the door.
3. The cross-user read row's operation name is widened to say what is read: the admins to notify
   of an appeal (ids) and review authors' names. The reason text is the plan's.

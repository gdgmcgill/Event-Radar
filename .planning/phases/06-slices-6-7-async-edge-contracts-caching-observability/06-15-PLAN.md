## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 6 goal, verbatim: "The asynchronous edge is credentialed and characterized, every handler validates its input, personalized responses stop being shareable by the CDN, and a failure in production becomes visible — closing Stage 3 with the behavior it started with." (Outcome-shaped, not a user story; surfaced, not invented. This plan proves the cache inversion end to end.)

<objective>
Prove, on the running app, that personalized responses are no longer shareable: regenerate the endpoint contract from the implemented classes, build a local shared-cache stand-in with positive and negative controls, run the cross-user test through it, and sweep every route's headers live.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 3's "a cross-user cache regression test passes"; F-025, F-026, DI-26, DI-58. Research Pitfall 10: without a shared cache, "B never sees A's data" passes trivially on `next start`, so the stand-in and its controls are what make the test mean something.

Output: the regenerated `endpoints.json`, `e2e/helpers/sharedCacheProxy.ts`, `e2e/specs/cross-user-cache.spec.ts`, `e2e/specs/cache-headers.spec.ts`, and two evidence files.
</objective>

</slice_outcome>

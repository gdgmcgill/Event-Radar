## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-03-01 | Denial of Service | deleting a cron route something still calls | mitigate | the caller census re-runs first and stops the task on any caller (Task 1 action; acceptance: `CRON_SECRET` readers gone only after the census) |
| T-06-03-02 | Spoofing | a wrong-length signature reaching a throw and a 500 (a length oracle) | mitigate | `verifySignature` length-checks before `timingSafeEqual`; wrong-length row expects 401 (Task 2 and 3 acceptance) |
| T-06-03-03 | Tampering | a captured valid payload replayed to insert again (F-039, High) | mitigate | `content_hash` populated; 23505 reported as duplicate; replay row in the handler suite (Task 3 acceptance) |

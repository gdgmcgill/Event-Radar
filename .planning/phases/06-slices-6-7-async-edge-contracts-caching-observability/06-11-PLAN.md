## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 6 goal, verbatim: "The asynchronous edge is credentialed and characterized, every handler validates its input, personalized responses stop being shareable by the CDN, and a failure in production becomes visible — closing Stage 3 with the behavior it started with." (Outcome-shaped, not a user story; surfaced, not invented. This plan delivers REFAC-16.)

<objective>
Reuse the contracts on the client: type every client-built mutation body from its contract, add development-only response checks to the 8 hooks, and gate the rule with a `--client` census (DEC-67, research Q5 and Pattern 2b).

Purpose: REFAC-16 ("zod contracts are exported and reused by client hooks so client and handler cannot drift") and ROADMAP criterion 2's last clause. Type-level reuse costs nothing in production; the development check catches response drift while developing.

Output: `src/lib/contractCheck.ts`, response schemas added to the contract files, 8 typed hooks, typed mutation call sites across components and pages, the `--client` census and its CI hook, and the bundle proof.
</objective>

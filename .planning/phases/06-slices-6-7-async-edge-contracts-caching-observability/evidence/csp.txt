INTENTIONAL BEHAVIOUR CHANGE: production pages no longer permit eval; local builds admit the local Supabase origin.

=== 1. What changed ===

next.config.js builds the policy in headers() from the environment the build sees (contentSecurityPolicy(env)):
  - script-src 'self' 'unsafe-inline', plus 'unsafe-eval' only when NODE_ENV === "development" (next dev);
  - NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL: when its origin is not covered by https://*.supabase.co, that origin joins img-src
    and connect-src and its ws/wss twin joins connect-src (production unchanged; harness gets 127.0.0.1:54321);
  - NEXT_PUBLIC_SENTRY_DSN: when it is an https DSN (key and project id), its ingest origin joins connect-src;
  - every other directive and every other header is byte-identical (compare the strings below).
'unsafe-inline' stays: the inline theme script (src/app/layout.tsx) and Next's inline bootstrap need it; the
nonce policy is DI-80 and F-055 stays Open for that half.
playwright.config.ts: bypassCSP: false; the long bypass comment replaced by a short one citing C13 and DEC-77;
SENTRY_DSN, NEXT_PUBLIC_SENTRY_DSN and SENTRY_AUTH_TOKEN blanked in the webServer env (as the Upstash names are).

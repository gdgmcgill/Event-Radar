# M0 critical-workflow certification coverage

Current implementation and observed local results, 2026-09-27. This reconciles the 16 [Validated workflows](../.planning/PROJECT.md) with the [research workflow/persona inventory](../.planning/research/FEATURES.md#table-stakes--persona--workflow-matrix). Overall certification and final suite totals belong in the [M0 report](../.planning/milestones/m0/REPORT.md).

The final clean-reset run in [playwright-final.txt](../.planning/milestones/m0/evidence/playwright-final.txt) passed **1,800 tests**, with **118 explicitly skipped non-McGill endpoint cells**. It includes the authoring, club, discovery/filter/recovery, participation, recommendation/analytics, upload and banned-admin cases below. The focused rerun passed 23/23. Intermediate logs are retained: the RSVP test previously asserted the deliberately unmaintained legacy `events.rsvp_count`; it now checks the authoritative RPC and active rows under DEC-31. Three page-smoke waits also failed before the readiness/redirect fixes. These intermediate failures are superseded by the final green run, not reinterpreted as passes.

“Browser” below means a rendered interaction and an asserted result. “Live API” means a request to the running application, sometimes followed by database checks of persisted effects. Seeded fixture insertion is setup, not evidence that the application can create that record. The [endpoint/page matrix](certification-matrix.md) supplies additional authorization and smoke coverage; malformed-body probes and page200s are not substitutes for successful workflows.

## Evidence boundaries and remaining actions

- The corrected RSVP, recovery and banned-admin cases passed in the final focused and combined runs. Intermediate logs and the corrected assertion rationale remain recorded above. No results in this document substitute for exact-commit CI, staging parity or the operational close-out in the M0 report.
- The 13-persona authorization matrix has ten real admitted personas, anonymous and machine requests, plus explicitly separate non-McGill admission coverage. Its 118 non-McGill endpoint cells are skipped by design. Workflow successes use the relevant permitted personas plus selected adversaries; **not every workflow × persona combination has a successful-path journey**.
- The fixture harness uses one worker and exact namespaces/cleanup. Profile snapshot/restore preserves content but permits normal `updated_at` changes. Scores and running experiments use explicit preconditions, and global functions have bounded safeguards. These are local test-isolation rules, not production restoration mechanisms.
- External OAuth/producer access, cloud Storage policy/configuration, remote CI/deployment/migrations, CDN isolation, Sentry/downtime receipt, staging load, rollback and backup restoration remain required external evidence.
- Lower-risk limitations remain disclosed rather than silently accepted: legacy RSVP export counter, existing tag/club-label presentation, invitation resend/GET acceptance, unsupported decline/member taxonomy, detailed private-profile presentation, per-path rate budgets/IP trust, and whole-service outage fallback. Their existing finding/deferred-item decisions remain authoritative.

This document maps current successful-path evidence and its limits. It does not declare foundation certification, invent an owner risk acceptance, or count boundary probes as complete product journeys.
